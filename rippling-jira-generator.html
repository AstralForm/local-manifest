SLAs vs Priority

There are SLAs implemented on each customer task created in engineering Jira boards. These SLAs vary by priority assigned to the Jira ticket.

Priority TTR SLA (Internal)
P0 - Highest (critical blockers listed above / sensitive escalations) 2 business days
P1 - High 7 business days
P2 - Medium 14 business days
P3 - Low 21 business days
P4 - Lowest NA (Support should set the right expectations with the customer and solve the case)
